[Stage 2, 5-10s] Continue: rainbow overhead. [Stage 3, 10-15s] Primary event: STICK points at each balloon in turn, left to right, bending his arm at the elbow, and KALYAKA nods its whole body once for each one. End state: STICK's finger on the third balloon, both smiling. Camera: slow Dolly In to a medium two-shot.
